HOW TO UPDATE:
==============

1. Delete old BounceAscent.exe from your Desktop
2. Copy new BounceAscent.exe from:
   \\wsl.localhost\Ubuntu\home\virus\bounce-ascent\exports
3. Run the new version!

Your save file will carry over automatically - all your stats are preserved!
