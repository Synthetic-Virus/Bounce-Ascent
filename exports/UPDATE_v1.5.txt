CONTROLS:
=========

Movement:
  ⬅️ Left:  A or Left Arrow
  ➡️ Right: D or Right Arrow

Jumping:
  ⬆️ Jump: Spacebar, W, or Up Arrow
  - Ball auto-jumps every 2.5 seconds
  - You can also force a jump anytime
  - Small cooldown between manual jumps

Other:
  - ESC: Quit to desktop (from game)
  - ESC: Close profile editor (from profile)

GAMEPLAY TIPS:
==============

1. Don't just rely on auto-jump!
   - Use manual jumps to fine-tune landings
   - Jump early to reach higher platforms

2. Move while in the air
   - Steer left/right while jumping
   - Line up with next platform

3. Watch the platform colors:
   - Green = Safe (static)
   - Yellow = Moving (time your jump)
   - Red = Breaks after landing (keep moving!)
   - Purple = Disappears (don't stay long!)

4. Don't fall behind the camera
   - Screen scrolls down
   - Stay near the top half of screen

5. Watch the background
   - Blue sky = Easy (height 0-50)
   - Sunset = Getting harder (50-100)
   - Dusk = Challenging (100-150)
   - Space = Expert mode! (150+)
