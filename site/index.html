Land on the beat.

Time your jump well and you climb two platforms instead of one, in the same amount of time. That is the whole game.

Most jumping games pick a jump strength and let the physics decide when you land. This one does the opposite: it picks the landing moment first, as an exact number of beats, then solves for the launch velocity that gets you there. Landing on a platform and landing on a beat are the same event, so the natural instinct, pressing jump the moment you touch down, is pressing on the beat.

A hazard line rises through the level from below and speeds up as you go. Ordinary timing outruns it early on. Later it does not.

Download

Windows build

Unsigned, so Windows will warn about it and some machines running Smart App Control will block it outright. iOS is in TestFlight.

Sound

Three tracks, and there is not a single audio file in the build. Everything is synthesised from note tables at runtime while you are in the menu, then cached. The first thing to do in game is Settings → Calibrate audio: output latency varies by more than 200 ms between wired headphones and Bluetooth, which is far wider than the 45 ms PERFECT window.
