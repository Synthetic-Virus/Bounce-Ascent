Before you write in

My timing feels wrong, or I never get PERFECT

Run Settings → Calibrate audio and tap sixteen times to the kick drum. This is the fix for the large majority of timing complaints, and it is not optional politeness: audio output latency varies by more than 200 ms between wired headphones and a Bluetooth speaker, while the PERFECT window is 45 ms wide. Uncalibrated, perfect human timing can still score badly through no fault of yours.

Recalibrate whenever you change headphones. Bluetooth in particular differs from wired by far more than the window.

I keep dying and I do not know why

A hazard line rises through the level from below and speeds up as you climb. Ordinary timing outruns it early on and does not later. Landing PERFECT climbs two platforms instead of one, in the same amount of time, so precise timing is how you stay ahead. See How to play on the menu.

The controls feel awkward on my phone

Tap anywhere to jump; hold the on-screen arrows to steer. Steering is disabled while you are standing on a platform, deliberately, so that lining up your next jump cannot walk you off the edge of the one you are on. Tilt steering is available under Settings → Controls if you prefer it.

The music is too loud

Settings → volume. Flashing effects, screen shake and vibration can each be turned off there too.

Reporting a bug

Useful details, in order of how much they help:

  • your device and iOS version
  • what you did immediately before it happened
  • whether it happens every time
  • for timing problems, whether you had calibrated first

Bug reports can also go to the issue tracker. Feature requests and roadmap suggestions are not being taken, which is not meant unkindly: the direction is already decided.
