Bounce Ascent

Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 August 2026

Bounce Ascent does not collect, transmit or store any personal data.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no accounts and no third-party SDKs of any kind.

Network access

The app makes no network connections. It does not contact us or anyone else, and it works identically with the device in airplane mode.

What is stored on your device

Two things, both written only to the app's own private storage on your device:

  • High scores, per music track
  • Settings, including volume, accessibility options and your audio latency calibration

Neither leaves your device. Deleting the app removes both, permanently, and we have no copy to delete because we never received one.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new date. Since the app collects nothing, any change would have to follow a change in the app itself.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to andrew@virusgaming.org, or see the support page.
